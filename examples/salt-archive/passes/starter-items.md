# Starter items

### Healing potion
- does: restores health
- idea: healing item
- carrier: white-salt
- because: tasting a calm memory steadies the body, which is the world's only magic
- name: Jar of Quiet Salt
- scene: `wren-calloway` in `the-salt-archive`, a jar uncorked, white light on her face
- flavor: "Someone's best afternoon, in a jar."
- alt: memory-lamp, heals the room instead of the drinker; the-great-drowning, too grim for a potion
- cites: white-salt, wren-calloway, the-salt-archive

### Area stun
- does: stops every enemy for one turn
- idea: area-wide effect that stops enemies
- carrier: q2-lamp-shows-the-drowning
- because: the whole market froze to watch the Drowning
- name: The Drowning Shown
- scene: `memory-lamp` at `low-tide-market`, a crowd lit by a projected wave
- flavor: "Nobody moved. Nobody could."
- alt: q1-brinewarden-seals-the-stack, a lock rather than a stun; saltborn, too small
- cites: q2-lamp-shows-the-drowning, memory-lamp, low-tide-market
