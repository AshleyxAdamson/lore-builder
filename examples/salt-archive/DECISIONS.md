# Decisions

Numbered, newest last. Each says what the owner settled and why. Entries cite these as `source: DECISIONS N`.

1. 2026-10-19: Original world, a small narrative RPG told in quests (codes Q1, Q2). The bible feeds quest design and item names. Tier means how central a thing is to a first playthrough.
2. 2026-10-19: Memories crystallize into salt when a person dies near the sea. Tasting the salt replays the memory. This is the one rule of magic; nothing else is magic.
3. 2026-10-19: The Brinewarden is not a villain. She seals memories to protect the living from grief, and the game never says she is wrong.
