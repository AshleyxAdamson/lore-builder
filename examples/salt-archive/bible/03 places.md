# 03 Places

## 1. Locations

### The Salt Archive
- slug: the-salt-archive
- aliases: the Archive
- what: A drowned cathedral where memory salt is shelved in sealed jars. Its lower stacks are underwater at high tide.
- appears: Q1, Q3
- tier: iconic
- maps-to: hub area, stealth section in the sealed stacks
- visual: Half-flooded cathedral nave, endless shelves of glowing salt jars, green water
- lighting: cold blue daylight from high windows, faint white glow from the jars
- source: owner

### Low Tide Market
- slug: low-tide-market
- what: A market on the rooftops that only opens when the tide is out.
- appears: Q2
- tier: known
- maps-to: shop, timed area that closes at high tide
- visual: Stalls on slate rooftops, rope bridges, lanterns, wet stone
- lighting: orange lanterns against a grey dusk
- source: owner
