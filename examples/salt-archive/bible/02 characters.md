# 02 Characters

# Section 1: Named characters

### Wren Calloway
- slug: wren-calloway
- aliases: Wren
- what: Apprentice archivist, seventeen, the player character. Breaks the Archive's first rule on her first day.
- appears: Q1, Q2, Q3
- tier: iconic
- maps-to: player character, curiosity as a mechanic (tasting salt unlocks dialogue)
- visual: Thin girl, cropped black hair, oversized grey archivist coat, salt-stained gloves, satchel
- source: owner

### The Brinewarden
- slug: the-brinewarden
- what: Keeper of the Salt Archive. Seals memories to spare the living their grief (DECISIONS 3).
- appears: Q1, Q3
- tier: iconic
- maps-to: boss fight that can end in talk, gatekeeper of locked areas
- visual: Tall old woman, white robes crusted with salt, iron key ring, calm face
- source: DECISIONS 3

### Old Tamsin
- slug: old-tamsin
- aliases: Tamsin
- what: Smuggler and market trader who sells memories the Archive never sealed. Knew Wren's mother (proposed).
- appears: Q2, Q3
- tier: known
- maps-to: merchant, quest giver for trade quests
- visual: Stout woman in a patched oilskin, gold teeth, many rings, pipe
- source: owner
