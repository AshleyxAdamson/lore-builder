# Original mode: facts come from the owner

The bible describes a world the owner is inventing. Canon is what the owner has said or approved. An agent's job is to record canon exactly and to **propose** around it, never to settle it.

## Sources

- `source: owner` for a fact the owner stated in conversation; `source: DECISIONS 12` for a fact settled in a numbered decision; `source: <document> p4` for a fact in one of the owner's documents listed in `bible/07 sources.md`.
- When the owner pastes notes, every fact in them is canon. Split the notes into entries and keep the owner's wording in `what` where it is already short.

## Proposals

- Anything you add to fill a gap (a name, a motive, a place, a visual line) carries `(proposed)` in the field you added. The build counts them.
- Offer two or three options for a gap that matters, each in a line, and let the owner pick. A pick becomes a decision in `DECISIONS.md` and the entry's `source` points at it; drop the `(proposed)` marker then.
- A proposal never contradicts canon. When the owner's facts contradict each other, write both, flag the conflict in your report, and ask which wins.

## Tier

How central the owner means a thing to be for the audience: `iconic` for what every player or reader will meet and remember, `known` for what engaged ones will, `deep-cut` for discoveries. Ask the owner when it is not obvious, and record the answer.

## Units

A world with no story yet can leave `[units] pattern` empty. Moments then group under plain `##` headings (an era, a region, a war) until the owner fixes the story's units.
